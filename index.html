Building, breaking, and understanding electronics.

I build independent electronics projects to understand how hardware and software actually work together — one problem, and one fix, at a time.

About me

I'm a 3rd-year Electrical Engineering student at Western University with a growing interest in embedded systems, robotics, and the connection between hardware and software.

I enjoy taking an idea from a concept to something physical — designing the system, writing the code, building the hardware, and figuring out why it doesn't work when it inevitably doesn't. Through my projects I'm developing my skills in electronics, programming, prototyping, and problem-solving while learning to approach engineering challenges more independently.

Why I build

My interest in electronics started with a simple curiosity: I wanted to make my ideas into something physical and understand the mechanics behind them. I began experimenting with individual components, writing my own code, and figuring out how hardware and software interact.

What started as curiosity turned into a habit of building, testing, troubleshooting, and trying again. Each project has given me a new problem to solve and a better understanding of how to approach the next one.
